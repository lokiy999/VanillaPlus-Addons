# VanillaPlus-Addons

A collection of small World of Warcraft addons for the **Vanilla+** 1.12 client. Some are originals; others were made by other creators. I made a small adjustment here and there to some of them for the Vanilla+ server.

Website: **https://lokiy.dev**

## Addons

| Addon | Original author | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| [SigmaProfessionFilter](SigmaProfessionFilter/) | [Sigma88](https://github.com/Sigma88/Sigma-ProfessionFilter) | Filtering, sorting, search and favourites for the profession windows, plus recipe info |

## Installing

1. Click **Code → Download ZIP** and extract it.
2. Open the folder of the addon you want and copy its contents (for example the `SigmaProfessionFilter*` folders) into `World of Warcraft/Interface/AddOns/`.
3. Restart the game or reload your UI.
